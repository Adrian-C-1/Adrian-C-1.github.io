#About Adrian

I’m Adrian. But that’s a boring name for a student about to get their bachelors degree and struggling to get a job in cyber. I also have a horse that does things (yes, a literal horse). He also likes programming.

#About the horse

The horse started programming about 6 years ago (as of writing this), and for the first 3 years he didn’t know how many areas there were, so he stuck with competitive programming in C++. After highschool he learned other things exist so he started learning about graphics and a lot of other programming languages. He is grateful for the knowledge gained from competitive programming, but he’s not touching that again.

How does a horse even write?

He uses his hooves, duh.

After about 1 year of learning graphics (sfml, opengl), gamedev(unreal, unity, he also read books) and web, he went to university, where he continued to learn software development and full stack, networking, math, as well as a more programming languages(rust, java, python, asm). He then pivoted to cybersecurity where he still currently learns. From the looks of it, this is the end-goal career.

How does a horse sit in a class in university?

He doesn’t sit as he would hurt his back on the wooden chairs.

He stands.

#About this notebook

The horse read a lot of articles written by people in order to learn, so he thinks it’s time to write his own ideas, problems and solves to those problems for other people to use in the future.

Is a horse against the GitHub tos?

No, as long as he follows the Terms of Service and doesn’t engage in automated bot-spamming or scraping.

#AI usage

The horse doesn’t like that AI is now a must have tool in this world but he has to live with it.

AI was used in designing this website as the horse absolutely sucks at design. All written content is entirely horse-made and human-narrated (unless specified).

End of the note. Beginning of the rabbit hole.
